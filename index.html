About

Hao Xue's research focus on applying machine learning and AI systems to biomedical informatics, including developing image-omics multimodal foundation models, natural language processing algorithms for extracting temporal information from Electronic Health Records (EHRs), statistical methods for multi-omics time-series pattern recognition, and Bayesian hierarchical models for multi-omics integration.

Prior to Cornell, he received his Master's degree in Biostatistics from Harvard University and his Bachelor's degree in Applied Mathematics (First Class with Honors) from Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University.
